Configuration Notes

Why include empty grades?

Since students see points (not percentages), including empty grades lets them watch their points accumulate toward 1000. This creates a sense of progression rather than showing an artificially high percentage early in the semester.

Student-Friendly Category Names (Natural Aggregation)

When using Natural (Sum of grades) aggregation, rename the top-level course category to "Course Points (X available)" where X is the total points possible (e.g., "Course Points (1000 available)"). Also rename the course total aggregation row to "Current Points Earned:" instead of "Course total". This makes it immediately clear to students how many points they can earn and how many they've accumulated.
