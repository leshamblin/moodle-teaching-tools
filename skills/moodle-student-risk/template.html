How statuses are calculated
🔴 Reach out
Two or more concerns triggered, or a single severe one (grade below %, or + assignments past due).
🟡 Check in
Exactly one concern triggered.
🟢 On track
No concerns triggered.
Stale-login concern
No course login in days (or days during the first days of term).
Past-due concern
At least one assignment past its due date with nothing submitted.
Low-grade concern
Course running grade below %. Skipped automatically if no graded work has been entered yet.
Low-engagement concern
Activity completion below half the cohort median. Only applies if completion tracking is enabled.
